# Company profile

Public research checked 2026-10-18. Source IDs resolve in [sources.md](sources.md).

Coffee Talk Cafe is a cafe and sandwich shop at **22 W Pennsylvania Ave, Suite 101, Towson, MD 21204**, telephone **410-296-1937**. Official Facebook, Instagram, Maps, Yelp and Grubhub identify the same Towson business. [S1–S4, S8]

Official accounts: [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/coffeetalktowson/) and [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/coffeetalktowson/). Maps uses Facebook as the business website. No standalone domain was verified. Do not associate coffeetalknepal.com with this business. [S1, S2, S8]

## Food and community

Coffee and espresso drinks, breakfast sandwiches, specialty sandwiches and wraps, salads, soups and locally made Taharka ice cream. The full researched menu is in [menu.json](menu.json). [S1, S4, S5]

The Towerlight's March 2022 feature describes a family-run business established in 2016 and names owner **Nara Khakurel**. Recent interior photos show a ten-year celebration. “Serving Towson since 2016” is supported. Current legal ownership was not independently reconfirmed beyond continuing official accounts. [S5, S6]

Official posts thank local customers and volunteers. The Towerlight describes a place to meet and catch up. Do not infer private event, reservation or room-hire services from that description. [S1, S6]

## Scope

One verified location; no delivery radius or additional branches established. Grubhub has an active matching order destination. The matching Uber Eats page says the cafe closed on that platform September 2, 2026. [S4, S11]
