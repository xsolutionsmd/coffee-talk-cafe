About us

Coffee Talk Cafe has been part of Towson since 2016. A family-run coffee and sandwich shop, it’s a place for a morning coffee, a lunch break and a little conversation.

Our menu brings together breakfast sandwiches, specialty sandwiches and wraps, salads, soups and locally made Taharka ice cream.
